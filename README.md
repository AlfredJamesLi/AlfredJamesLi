# Guojing Li | 李国静

**NLP · Large Language Models · AI-supported Assessment**

I am a PhD student in Human Resource Management at the School of Labor and Human Resources, Renmin University of China, supervised by Professor Wenxia Zhou. I am also undertaking joint PhD training in Data Science at City University of Hong Kong, supervised by Professor Xiangyu Zhao, and am affiliated with the [Applied Machine Learning Lab (AML)](https://aml-cityu.github.io/team/).

My research combines natural language processing, large language models, and psychometrics to study how text can support assessment and decision-making. I work on competency extraction, structured feedback, and recommendation tools, with applications in recruitment and career development.

[Personal website](https://alfredjamesli.github.io/AlfredJamesLi/) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-2948-2220) · [RUC email](mailto:alfredjameslee@ruc.edu.cn) · [CityU email](mailto:guojingli3-c@my.cityu.edu.hk)

## Research Interests

- **NLP & large language models:** Text mining and information extraction from recruitment texts, open-ended responses, and other real-world corpora.
- **AI-supported assessment:** Interpretable text-based assessment, structured feedback, and multi-source evaluation.
- **Intelligent applications:** Job recommendation, competency extraction, and career-development support.
